STATION 01

Before you take the offer

What pass this salary can get you, and whether your family can come along

EP minimum fixed monthly salary (current)

From S$5,600 for most sectors, rising with age (S$10,700 at 45 and above); financial services from S$6,200 (S$11,800 at 45 and above). The threshold counts fixed monthly salary only; variable bonuses do not count.

Note: from 2027-01-01, new applications need S$6,000 (general) / S$6,600 (financial services). If you are negotiating an offer now but filing next year, the new thresholds apply to you.

Meeting the salary bar ≠ approval: COMPASS needs 40 points

Four foundational criteria (salary, qualifications, nationality diversity, local employment) plus two bonus criteria; you pass at 40 points. Three exemptions: fixed monthly salary of S$22,500 or more, intra-corporate transferees, and short-term roles of one month or less.

The official pre-check, the Self-Assessment Tool (SAT), is run on the employer side (HR) via the EP eService, so at the interview you can simply ask: have you run the SAT?

S Pass: your threshold, and your employer's cost

Minimum fixed monthly salary for new applications: S$3,300 (financial services S$3,800); from 2027-01-01 this rises to S$3,600/S$4,000.

The employer also faces a quota (10% of the whole firm in services) and a monthly levy (a flat S$650 from 2025-09-01; the old tier system is abolished). That is the real cost structure behind an employer hesitating to hire you on an S Pass, and it is useful to know when negotiating the offer.

SG · MOMThresholdsLevyCaptured 2026-08-06

Can your family come along: two salary bars

Spouse and unmarried children under 21 (Dependant's Pass, DP): the main applicant needs a fixed monthly salary of S$6,000. Parents (Long-Term Visit Pass, LTVP): S$12,000. Both look at your individual fixed monthly salary; year-end and variable bonuses do not count, and household income is not combined.

A DP carries no automatic right to work: for your spouse to work in Singapore, an employer must separately apply for an EP/S Pass or a Work Permit for DP holders; the Letter of Consent (LOC) is only for DP holders running their own business. If your financial plan assumes two incomes, clear this hurdle before you count on it.

Aiming for sustainability work? Singapore is officially pushing this track

The Singapore Green Plan 2030 is a whole-of-government national agenda, and the Green Economy is one of its five pillars, with net zero by 2050 as the long-term goal. Sustainability roles therefore span finance, energy and corporate sustainability teams, making this a track worth evaluating for Taiwanese professionals heading to Singapore.

STATION 02

From application to IPA

After the employer files: what you are waiting for, and for how long

Applications go through the EP eService; timing depends on where the company sits

Singapore-registered companies: processed or updated within 10 working days; overseas employers with no local entity: most within 6 weeks.

SG · MOMOfficial sourceCaptured 2026-08-06

The IPA letter is your entry approval, valid for 6 months

On approval you first receive an In-Principle Approval (IPA) letter; enter Singapore and complete card collection within 6 months. The IPA is not the pass card yet: registration and card collection come after arrival (see the next station).

SG · MOMOfficial sourceCaptured 2026-08-06

Medical exam: whatever the IPA letter says is the rule

The official EP/S Pass eligibility pages carry no blanket medical-exam requirement (that rule belongs to the Work Permit); whether you need one, and what it covers, is stated in your IPA letter. Clinic marketing pages are not an official basis.

STATION 03

First 30 days on the ground

Order matters: arrival card → pass card → Singpass → bank account, SIM

SG Arrival Card: within 3 days before arrival, free

Submit online within 3 days of arrival, counting arrival day itself. It is free, and the only official portal is eservices.ica.gov.sg; the many paid "agent" sites crowding search results are imitations.

SG · ICAOfficial sourceCaptured 2026-08-06

Card collection: register within 2 weeks; the card arrives in 5 working days

Within 2 weeks of pass issuance, visit the Employment Pass Services Centre (EPSC) for fingerprinting and photo; the card is then delivered within 5 working days. Once registered for Singpass you can use the digital pass in the SGWorkPass app in the meantime.

SG · MOMOfficial sourceCaptured 2026-08-06

Bank accounts: three banks, three rulebooks; do not assume they match

DBS opens accounts on the IPA letter (no need to wait for the physical card); OCBC's remote onboarding does not cover Taiwan passports (Hong Kong, Malaysia, Indonesia and mainland China only), and its in-branch route means passport + valid pass + one proof of address, with an initial deposit of S$1,000; UOB is in-branch only and accepts original documents only.

SG · Bank sitesDBSOCBCUOBCaptured 2026-08-06

Mobile SIM: passport registration lasts only 30 days

From 2024-07-15, prepaid SIMs registered on a foreign passport stay valid for 30 days and must be re-registered with a Singapore document (your work pass) before the deadline, or they are cut off with no refund of remaining value; the cap is 3 prepaid cards per person. Get a short-term card on landing, then return to a store to re-register once you hold your pass.

The original IMDA announcement page is not machine-readable; the figures are cross-verified against the supervising ministry's (MDDI) page and the telco's official terms.

SG · MDDI/telcosMDDISingtel termsCaptured 2026-08-06

The exact window for first-time Singpass registration

FIN holders can register in the app with face verification; but the official wording on when after issuance you may register (whether you must wait for the physical card) has not been obtained, so this is being verified and no answer is listed. Tax filing and most online services sit behind Singpass, so set it up as soon as you land.

STATION 04

The first year

Tax on both sides, Taiwan NHI and household registry, renting, driving licence, remittance

Singapore tax: 183 days decides who you are

≥183 days in Singapore in the preceding calendar year = tax resident, on progressive rates with the first S$20,000 tax-free and a top rate of 24%; non-residents pay 15% on employment income or the resident rates, whichever is higher. Holders of a work pass valid ≥1 year are treated as residents from the start, re-assessed at tax clearance when you leave: if you are under 183 days by then, the whole period is re-settled as a non-resident.

So quitting before the half-year mark is a money question, not just a career one; and once the pass is cancelled you only get a short legal stay. Run the tax numbers before you pick a leaving date.

SG · IRASTax ratesResidency rulesCaptured 2026-08-06

Taiwan NHI (健保): the overseas suspension option is abolished; stop following old guides

From 2024-12-23 the NHI Administration no longer accepts coverage suspension; while working abroad, premiums keep being paid without interruption. Nearly every online tutorial about suspending NHI before leaving to save premiums describes the pre-abolition regime.

Coverage only ends if your household registration is moved out; the rules for rejoining on return are on the Return Line. This section cites only current NHIA documents.

TW · NHIAOfficial Q&A PDFCaptured 2026-08-06

Taiwan household registration (戶籍): 2 years abroad and it is moved out, not revoked

Household Registration Act, Article 16: after 2 years abroad, move-out registration applies (the household registration office may process it directly after notice). Move-out does not affect nationality or the validity of your ID card, and you can re-register the very day you re-enter Taiwan; but it does trigger NHI disenrolment and loss of voting eligibility.

TW · Law/MOIStatuteMOI explainerCaptured 2026-08-06

Taiwan tax: the NT$1,000,000 overseas-income threshold, plus the treaty timing gap

Once your filing household's combined overseas income reaches NT$1,000,000 (NT$100 萬) in a year, the full amount counts toward basic income (AMT), with an exemption of NT$7,500,000 (NT$750 萬, applicable for ROC tax years 113–115) and a 20% rate. The new Taiwan–Singapore tax agreement only applies from 2027-01-01; until then the 1981 treaty still governs. News that a new treaty is signed does not mean it is in force.

Renting (HDB): 6-month minimum, plus three checks you run yourself

Whole flat or a room: minimum 6 months; your pass must still have ≥6 months validity on the landlord's application date; occupancy caps are 6 for 3-room flats and 8 for 4-room or larger (reverting to 6 after end-2028). The official advice for tenants protecting themselves:

  1. Ask for proof of the owner's identity and ownership
  2. Ask for the HDB rental approval, with the lease stating it is void without approval
  3. After approval, use the official e-Service to confirm you are listed as an authorised occupant

Condos (private housing) follow URA rules instead: each occupant must stay at least 3 consecutive months, and homes under 90 sqm are capped at 6 occupants (90 sqm and above temporarily relaxed to 8 until end-2028); tenants do not register with URA.

Rental scams: never pay just to view or to reserve a flat

January–October 2024: police received 430+ reports with losses over S$2.7 million (S$270 萬). The standard play: fake listings + a real agent's licence number + WhatsApp pressure for a deposit. Protect yourself: look up the phone number shown on the ad in the CEA public register, view the flat in person and verify the agent's identity, and remember that arranging viewings and securing a tenancy must not be charged to you.

SG · SPF/CEAPolice advisoryCheck an agentCaptured 2026-08-06

Market rent: use official median transacted rents, not an agent's word

HDB publishes median rents by town and flat type, and officially says they can serve as a reference benchmark before you sign. Check the numbers before viewing; skip anything priced absurdly above them.

SG · HDBRental statisticsCaptured 2026-08-06

Remitting money back to Taiwan: the quota is huge, the reporting bar is low

Taiwan individuals get an annual cumulative FX conversion quota of US$10,000,000 (1,000 萬美元, effective 2024-11-01; the old US$5 million cap no longer applies); any single transaction reaching the equivalent of NT$500,000 (NT$50 萬) must be declared. Declared, not taxed; do not drift into underground remittance just to skip paperwork.

TW · CBCQuota announcementDeclaration FAQCaptured 2026-08-06

Driving licence: convert within 12 months; non-English licences need a translation

Staying beyond 12 months means converting to a Singapore licence: a S$50 conversion fee and passing the Basic Theory Test (BTT); per the official document checklist, non-English licences (including Taiwan's) need a translation issued by a Singapore-registered translation company.

Two details have no official wording and are being verified: when the 12 months starts counting, and whether any theory-test exemption exists. Call the Traffic Police to confirm before you go.

STATION 05

Putting down roots

Whether and when to apply for PR; your pay structure changes once you have it

PR application: official timeline within 6 months; sync it against your EP expiry

PR is handled by ICA (apply online via e-PR): with complete documents, processing takes within 6 months, longer for some cases. Fees: S$100 to apply; on approval, Entry Permit S$20 + 5-year Re-Entry Permit (REP) S$50 + identity card S$50. Doing it yourself costs about S$220 end to end; agents often charge ten times that, with benefits you cannot verify.

A pitfall that has actually happened: the EP expiry and the PR decision land days apart, the EP lapses first, and the PR application dies with it. EP renewal and PR are two independent timelines; never bet the PR outcome against your EP expiry date.

SG · ICAOfficial application pageCaptured 2026-08-06

EP/S Pass holders pay no CPF; contributions start the day you become a PR

CPF contributions apply only to citizens and PRs. The first two years as a PR run on graduated rates (year 1: employer 4% + employee 5%; year 2: 9%＋15%), then the full 17%＋20% from year 3 (below age 55, on wages up to S$8,000 a month). Model your take-home pay before taking PR.

SG · CPFEmployer obligations2026 rate tableCaptured 2026-08-06

Before using SRS to save tax, learn its exit rules

Foreigners can contribute up to S$35,700 a year, tax-deductible; but withdrawing before retirement age means full taxation plus a 5% penalty. If the plan is to quit, cash out and leave, this is the landmine. Foreigners who have held the account for 10 years may withdraw in full without penalty (this detail is under review). Accounts: DBS/OCBC/UOB.

This site gives no investment advice: whether to open an SRS account is your call; this card only makes sure you know the exit rules before you do.

RETURN LINE

Leaving Singapore, returning to Taiwan

Most people walk this line after 2 to 5 years: exit tax clearance, NHI re-enrolment, household registration

IR21 tax clearance: your last month's pay gets withheld, by design, not by a difficult employer

When a foreign employee resigns, is posted overseas, or leaves Singapore for more than 3 months: the employer must file Form IR21 at least 1 month in advance and withhold all monies due (salary, bonus, leave encashment) from the day it knows, until IRAS clears you; failing to file without reasonable excuse carries a fine of up to S$5,000. e-Filed cases are processed within 7 working days. Budget this into your resignation talks and your cash flow.

SG · IRASOfficial pageStep-by-step guideCaptured 2026-08-06

Rejoining NHI on return: the new rule keys on your disenrolment date, not the old idea of 4 years abroad

Restore household registration within 2 years of the disenrolment date: coverage resumes the same day. Beyond 2 years: coverage resumes only after 6 full months of restored registration (for those employed on return, from the employment start date). The 4-years-abroad framing you see online is the 2021 regime.

TW · NHIAOfficial Q&A PDFCaptured 2026-08-06

Restoring household registration: doable the day you arrive

After entering on your ROC passport, bring your ID card and household certificate (戶口名簿) to the household registration office of the place you are moving into, and the move-in registration is completed the same day. Restored registration is the prerequisite for NHI re-enrolment and most other rights.
